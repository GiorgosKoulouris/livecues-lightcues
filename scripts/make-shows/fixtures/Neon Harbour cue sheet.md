## Rig

Built for the venue's 14 Fixtures. Parts lit: front, back, floor, beams.

- Part left out: fx. The rig has no Fixtures for it.
- 2 front washes are movers. Movement, Pulse and Colour FX leave the front out, so they hold still.

## Colour Sets

Fire one at bar 1 of each song. Rules take their colours from the held set by slot: Primary on the back, Secondary on the floor, Accent (white) on the beams. The first holds when the Show opens. The front is pale kelvin white and steady.

## Tier guide

Names follow `<Tier>: <Name>`. Tiers sit on two octaves each: Chill C1 (24), Groove C3 (48), Drive C5 (72), Peak C7 (96), on every Layer that has tier Scenes. Utilities are on notes 12–23 of the Look channel, Hits on notes 12 and up of channel 5. Note 0 releases a Layer.

| Tier | Use | Dynamic Layers at once |
|---|---|---|
| Chill | Verses, intros and ballads. At most two parts lit, slow fades. | 1 |
| Groove | Mid-energy verses and pre-choruses. | 2 |
| Drive | Choruses. Beams come in. | 2 |
| Peak | The last chorus and the big moments. Everything lit, snapped. | 3 |

## How the Layers combine

- Look (channel 1): colour and intensity. Each Layer holds one Scene at a time.
- Movement (channel 2), Pulse (channel 3) and Colour FX (channel 4) add one kind of Effect each. They never touch the front. Colour FX use slot colours only. Each tier offers them on as many Layers as its stacking cap, so one of each at once stays inside it. A tier's Still Scene runs no Effect and does not count.
- Hit (channel 5): Flash only, held for 2 beats or less. A Hit may flash faster than the cap. Release restores what was under it.
- Every Look and Effect is built from slots, so a Colour Set change recolours it at once, with the Scenes still active.
- The front is steady and never moves or pulses. Fades are in beats.
- Scenes target Roles and Zones only, so the Show runs in any venue.
- Movement is stepped up in length so it stays under the Speed limit at 154 BPM, the fastest song.

## Recommended combos

Fire one Scene per Layer. Keep to the cap for the tier: Chill 1 dynamic Layer, Groove 2, Drive 2, Peak 3.

| Section | Look | Movement | Pulse / Colour FX | Hits |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Intro | Walk-on, then Reveal on the vocal | Chill: Sway or Chill: Still | none | none |
| Verse 1 | Chill Looks | Chill: Sway or Chill: Still | none (Chill allows 1 dynamic Layer) | none |
| Verse 2+ | Groove Looks | Groove: Wave or Groove: Still | Groove: Slow Fade | none |
| Pre-chorus, build | Groove, then Drive | Groove: Still or Drive: Still | Drive: Beat Pulse | Hit: Riser in the last bar |
| Chorus | Drive Looks | Drive: Fan or Drive: Texture | Drive: Beat Pulse or Drive: Beam Square | Hit: Blinder on beat 1 |
| Last chorus, drop | Peak Looks | Peak: Wide Fan or Peak: Mirror Circle | Peak: Beat Pulse + Peak: Colour Step (3 Layers at most) | Hit: Blinder + Hit: Strobe at the end |
| Bridge | Groove Looks, the other Colour Set slot swapped | Groove: Wave | Groove: Slow Fade | none |
| Break, breakdown | Silhouette or Shafts | Chill: Still | none | none |
| Solo | Drive Look minus one part | Drive: Fan | none | none |
| Outro | Chill Looks, warm and low | Chill: Still | none | Bow on the last hit |
| Stop | Dark | — | — | Hit: Blackout |

## Strobe

Strobe cap: 4 Hz for every Scene fired by Go.

No Scene fired by Go flashes faster than the cap at 154 BPM, the fastest song.

Hits that flash faster than the cap. They are Flash only, so the cap does not apply. Hold for 2 beats or less:

- Hit: Strobe
- Hit: Roll
