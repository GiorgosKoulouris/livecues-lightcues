## Colour Sets

Rules take their colours from the held set by slot. Looks put Primary on the back, Secondary on the floor and Accent on the beams, and the front takes slot colours like any part. The first set holds when the Show opens. Names describe the colours (Primary / Secondary / Accent). Each song has three roles:

- Main: fire at bar 1. Held through verses and choruses.
- Break: fire on a bridge or breakdown, then Main again. Leads with a contrast hue.
- Swap: fire on the last chorus. The Main with Primary and Secondary exchanged.

## Tier guide

Names follow `<Tier>: <Name>`. Tiers sit on two octaves each: Chill C1 (24), Groove C3 (48), Drive C5 (72), Peak C7 (96), on every Layer that has tier Scenes. Utilities are on notes 12–23 of the Look channel, Hits on notes 12 and up of channel 5. Note 0 releases a Layer.

| Tier | Use | Dynamic Layers at once |
|---|---|---|
| Chill | Verses, intros and ballads. At most two parts lit, slow fades. | 2 |
| Groove | Mid-energy verses and pre-choruses. | 3 |
| Drive | Choruses. Beams come in. | 3 |
| Peak | The last chorus and the big moments. Everything lit, snapped. | 4 |

## How the Layers combine

- Look (channel 1): colour and intensity. Each Layer holds one Scene at a time.
- Movement (channel 2), Pulse (channel 3) and Colour FX (channel 4) add Effects. Every tier offers all three. Movement leaves the front out (M1); Pulse and Colour FX take any part, the front included, or everything. Colour FX use slot colours only. A Move+FX Movement Scene adds an intensity Effect on the beams. A tier's Still Scene runs no Effect and does not count. The stacking cap is per cue: fire one Scene per Layer and add up the Effects.
- Hit (channel 5): Flash only, held for 2 beats or less. A Hit may flash faster than the cap. Release restores what was under it.
- Every Look and Effect is built from slots, so a Colour Set change recolours it at once, with the Scenes still active.
- The front takes slot colours in Looks, Pulse and Colour FX. Fades are in beats.
- Scenes target Roles and Zones only, so the Show runs in any venue.
- Movement is stepped up in length so it stays under the Speed limit at 154 BPM, the fastest song.

## Strobe

Strobe cap: 4 Hz for every Scene fired by Go.

No Scene fired by Go flashes faster than the cap at 154 BPM, the fastest song.

Hits that flash faster than the cap. They are Flash only, so the cap does not apply. Hold for 2 beats or less:

- Hit: Strobe
- Hit: Roll
